Privacy Policy for MOCKBIRD

Effective Date: November 24, 2026 • Developer: Abhinab Das

Summary: MOCKBIRD is an offline-first quantitative aptitude preparation app. We do not require account registration, passwords, or third-party social login. Your study progress and local display name stay on your device. Third-party services are used strictly for serving ads (Google AdMob), processing purchases (Google Play Billing), and protecting coin/pass integrity (Vault Sync).

1. Information We Collect & Store

  • Local Study Data & Display Name (On-Device Only): Your optional display name, XP, practice progress, mock test history, and Maths Studio notes/workspaces are stored locally on your device. We do not collect or transmit your personal profile to our servers.
  • Anonymous Device / Vault Identifier (Vault Sync): To prevent tampering with promotional coins and temporary PRO pass timers across app resets, MOCKBIRD synchronizes a pseudonymous device/installation identifier and coin/pass state with our backend Vault service (hosted on Cloudflare). This identifier is not linked to your real-world identity, email address, or phone number.
  • In-App Purchases (Google Play Billing): Purchases of Gold Coin packs and Lifetime PRO are processed entirely by Google Play. We receive purchase verification tokens and SKU status from Google Play to unlock your entitlements; we never access or store your UPI ID, credit/debit card details, or billing address.
  • Advertising Data (Google AdMob): In Free Mode and during ad-supported Silver PRO passes, MOCKBIRD displays ads via Google AdMob. Google and its ad partners may collect your Android Advertising ID, IP address, coarse location, device characteristics, and ad interaction metrics to serve and measure ads. Ad requests are disabled for active Gold Coin passes and Lifetime PRO users.

2. How We Use Information

  • Deliver offline quantitative practice, timed mocks, Maths Lab fusion challenges, and the Maths Postmortem Studio.
  • Verify and restore Google Play purchases and prevent fraudulent manipulation of earned or promotional coin balances.
  • Display ads to support free access and measure ad delivery health.

3. Third-Party Services

MOCKBIRD relies on the following third-party providers, each governed by its own privacy policy:

  • Google Privacy Policy & Google AdMob / Advertising Policies
  • Google Payments Privacy Notice
  • Cloudflare Privacy Policy

4. Your Choices, Opt-Out & Data Deletion

  • Reset or Delete Advertising ID: You can reset or delete your Android Advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads anytime in Android Settings → Google → Ads.
  • Remove Ads Permanently: Upgrading to Lifetime PRO (or activating a Gold Coin pass) disables Banner and Interstitial ads inside MOCKBIRD.
  • Delete Local Data: Clear all locally stored study progress and display name at any time via Android Settings → Apps → MOCKBIRD → Storage → Clear storage or by uninstalling the app.
  • Request Vault Record Deletion: To request deletion of any anonymous Vault sync record associated with your device, email us at the address below.

5. Children's Privacy

MOCKBIRD is designed for graduate and competitive-exam aspirants (such as CAT Quant preparation) and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal data to us, contact us so we can promptly remove it.

6. Security & Policy Updates

All network communication for billing verification, ad requests, and Vault synchronization is encrypted in transit using HTTPS/TLS. We may update this Privacy Policy periodically; changes take effect upon posting to this page with a revised Effective Date.
